# Sprint 1 Retrospective

## What Went Well

Planning the Sprint 1 stories in more detail helped me understand the work better. Rewriting the backlog items as full user stories and adding acceptance criteria made the requirements clearer and made it easier to understand what would need to be tested.

Using story points also helped me compare the complexity of the different stories. I realised that some features, such as viewing subject progress, would require more work than I originally expected.

## What Didn't Go Well

Some of the original backlog items were too simple and did not explain enough about what the user actually needed or why they needed it. This meant that more planning was required before the stories were clear enough to develop.

I also found estimating the stories difficult at first because the original backlog phrases did not contain enough detail to judge their complexity accurately.

## Change for Sprint 2

For Sprint 2, I will write each backlog item as a complete user story with clear acceptance criteria before adding it to the sprint. I will then estimate its story points using the completed stories from Sprint 1 as references before deciding whether it is small and clear enough to include.
